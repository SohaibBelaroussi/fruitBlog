Simple Fruit Recipes

Good fruit barely needs a recipe, but a little inspiration never hurts. Here are three easy, fruit-forward recipes you can put together with a handful of ingredients and a few minutes in the kitchen.

🥤 Berry Banana Smoothie

Ingredients:

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 cup mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 cup milk or yogurt
  • 1 tsp honey (optional)

Steps:

  1. Peel the banana and add it to a blender.
  2. Add the mixed berries, milk or yogurt, and honey.
  3. Blend on high until smooth and creamy.
  4. Pour into a glass and serve immediately.

🥗 Simple Mixed Fruit Salad

Ingredients:

  • 1 apple, chopped
  • 1 orange, peeled and segmented
  • 1 cup grapes, halved
  • 1 cup pineapple chunks
  • 1 tbsp lime juice

Steps:

  1. Wash and prepare all the fruit, cutting it into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Combine the apple, orange, grapes, and pineapple in a large bowl.
  3. Drizzle with lime juice and toss gently to combine.
  4. Chill for 10 minutes before serving.

🍎 Baked Cinnamon Apples

Ingredients:

  • 4 apples, cored and sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Arrange the apple slices in a baking dish.
  3. Drizzle with melted butter and sprinkle with brown sugar and cinnamon.
  4. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until the apples are tender.
  5. Let cool slightly before serving.
