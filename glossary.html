Fruit Glossary

Fruit talk comes with its own vocabulary. Whether you've spotted a term like phytonutrient on a nutrition label or wondered what makes a stone fruit a stone fruit, this glossary breaks down 15 fruit-related terms in plain language.

Key Terms

Antioxidant

A compound found in many fruits that helps protect the body's cells from damage caused by free radicals.

Bromelain

An enzyme found in pineapples that helps break down protein and is known for aiding digestion and reducing inflammation.

Citrus Fruit

A family of fruits, including oranges, lemons, and limes, prized for their tangy flavor and high Vitamin C content.

Fiber

A plant-based nutrient found in fruit skins and flesh that supports healthy digestion and helps you feel full.

Flavonoid

A group of plant pigments responsible for many fruits' vibrant colors, also linked to heart and brain health benefits.

Peak Season

The window of time when a fruit is harvested at its ripest, offering the best possible flavor, texture, and nutrition.

Phytonutrient

A natural compound produced by plants, including fruits, that can support overall health beyond basic vitamins and minerals.

Pome

A type of fruit, like apples and pears, with a core of small seeds surrounded by a firm, edible flesh.

Resveratrol

An antioxidant compound found in grapes and their skins, often associated with supporting heart health.

Ripening

The natural process by which a fruit develops its full sweetness, color, and softness after being harvested or while still on the plant.

Rind

The tough outer skin of certain fruits, such as citrus and melons, that protects the flesh inside.

Stone Fruit

A fruit, such as a peach, cherry, or plum, that has a single hard pit or "stone" at its center.

Superfruit

An informal term for fruits, like blueberries and strawberries, that are especially rich in nutrients and antioxidants.

Tropical Fruit

A fruit, such as a mango, pineapple, or coconut, that grows in warm, humid climates near the equator.

Vitamin C

An essential nutrient abundant in many fruits that supports the immune system and helps the body absorb iron.
